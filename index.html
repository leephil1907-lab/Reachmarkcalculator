Install on iPhone / iPad

Add Reachmark directly to your home screen for instant full-screen offline calculations:

1 Tap the Share button in Safari's bottom toolbar.
2 Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
3 Tap Add in the top-right. Reachmark will appear as an app icon!

Install on Android

Install Reachmark directly to your phone for full-screen offline calculations:

1 Tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right of Chrome.
2 Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
3 Tap Install to confirm. It will install into your phone's app list!
